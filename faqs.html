SEO (Search Engine Optimization) FAQ

1. What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of optimizing a website to rank better in search engines like Google.

2. How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO results typically take 3 to 6 months, depending on competition and website status.

3. What are On-Page, Off-Page, and Technical SEO?

  • On-Page SEO: Optimizing content, keywords, images, and URL structure.
  • Off-Page SEO: Building backlinks, social signals, and brand mentions.
  • Technical SEO: Improving site speed, mobile-friendliness, sitemaps, etc.

4. How to do keyword research?

Use tools like Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Ubersuggest to find low-competition, high-volume keywords.

5. What is a backlink, and what type is good?

  • Good Backlink: Natural links from high-authority sites.
  • Bad Backlink: Spammy links from low-quality sites.

Facebook Ads FAQ

1. What is Facebook Ads?

Facebook Ads are paid advertisements displayed on Facebook to reach a targeted audience.

2. How do Facebook Ads work?

Facebook Ads use algorithms to reach specific audiences based on demographics, location, interests, and behaviors.

3. What is the difference between Boost Post and Ads?

  • Boost Post: Increases reach for an existing post.
  • Facebook Ads: Offers advanced targeting and customization options.

4. How much budget is required for Facebook Ads?

It depends on your goal and conversions, but you can start with $5-$10 per day.

5. What is Audience Targeting?

Targeting based on location, age, gender, interest, behavior, Custom Audience, and Lookalike Audience.

6. What are CPC, CPM, and CTR?

  • CPC (Cost Per Click): Cost per ad click.
  • CPM (Cost Per Mille): Cost per 1,000 impressions.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.

7. What is Facebook Pixel, and how to set it up?

Facebook Pixel is a tracking code that tracks traffic and conversions. It can be created in Facebook Business Manager and installed on a website.

8. Why do Facebook Ads get rejected?

  • Violating community guidelines.
  • Excessive text usage.
  • Promoting restricted content (alcohol, tobacco, etc.).
  • Placing ads in the wrong category.
